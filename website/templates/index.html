What it watches

Know which phase your pile is in.

A healthy pile warms up, peaks, cools and matures over about seven weeks. CompostIQ reads the temperature and its 24-hour trend to place your pile on that curve, so you are never guessing.

  1. 01

    Early mesophilic

    Microbes wake up and the pile starts to warm.

    28–45 °C · 1–3 days

  2. 02

    Active thermophilic

    Heat-loving bacteria take over and the pile heats fast.

    45–60 °C · 2–4 days

  3. 03

    Peak decomposition

    The hottest stretch. Three days at 55 °C kills pathogens.

    57–65 °C · 4–7 days

  4. 04

    Cooling

    Activity slows and the pile cools back down.

    30–60 °C · 5–10 days

  5. 05

    Maturation

    Fungi and worms finish the job. Nearly ready to use.

    28–32 °C · 24–36 days

How it works

Plug in, pair, and let the pile talk.

  1. 1{{ icon('device', 'icon icon--lg') }}

    Put a sensor in the bin

    Push the probe into the middle of the pile and power it up. It joins your Wi-Fi and starts reading straight away.

  2. 2{{ icon('link', 'icon icon--lg') }}

    Pair it with a code

Alerts

It tells you what to do, not just what it measured.

When a reading drifts out of range, CompostIQ raises an alert with the fix. Mark it done and a short “stabilising” note takes its place until the readings settle.

  • Temperature > 65 °CTurn the pile to release heatHigh
  • Oxygen < 2 %Turn or aerate. It is going anaerobicHigh
  • Moisture < 40 %Add water, about 3 L per 100 L of materialMedium
  • Moisture > 65 %Mix in dry browns: leaves, cardboardMedium
  • No reading for 2 intervalsCheck the device's power and Wi-FiMedium
  • ≥ 55 °C for 3 daysPathogen-kill temperature reachedGood news
Open data

Every reading, anonymised and open to researchers.

Readings from CompostIQ bins feed a public dataset. Names, locations and device IDs are stripped out. What is left is the science: how real piles heat up, dry out and breathe.
